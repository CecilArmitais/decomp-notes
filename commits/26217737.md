## No invented types, and why that is checkable

**Fact.** `struct dse_track` and `struct dse_sub_loop` already exist in
`lib/DSE/include/dse.h`, and **every offset these functions touch lines up with a
named field there** -- `is_playing` at 2, `wait_ticks_left` at `0xc`,
`previous_wait_ticks` at `0x10`, `loop_start` at `0x20`.

That alignment is itself evidence: an offset-by-offset match against an
independently written header is a much stronger signal than any single function.

## The shared signature is read off the dispatcher, not guessed

**Fact.** `ParseDseEvent` loads the handler from `SMD_EVENTS_FUN_TABLE` and calls
it with `r0` already advanced past the opcode byte, `r1` its own first argument,
`r2` the track, and `r3` `track->channel` from offset `0x54`; the returned `r0`
becomes the track's new position. Its caller passes a sequence and that
sequence's track at offset `0xa8`, which is what types the second parameter.

**Explicitly flagged as weak evidence at the time:** the second parameter is
**unused by all seven bodies**, so their own bytes do not constrain its type. It
is typed from the dispatcher's call site alone.

That gap is closed one commit later -- see [`00d4d642`](00d4d642.md), where
`EndTrack` writes through it.
