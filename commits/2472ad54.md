**Fact.** `AllocUnkBagStruct` and `FreeUnkBagStruct` manage the pointer at offset
8 of `UNK_STORAGE_STRUCT_0XC` -- the field the selection accessors in
[`80c33c35`](80c33c35.md) read.

**Fact, and a satisfying cross-check.** The allocation is `0xC8` bytes, which is
`0x32` words -- **the same count `ClearBagSelectedItemTable` zeroes** and the
same inventory size the bag walkers use. Three independent sightings of the same
constant.

## A second declaration, flagged rather than hidden

**Fact.** `MemAlloc` is still asm and has no owning header, so its prototype is
provisional and declared beside the caller, **matching the signature
`overlay_31_02382820.c` already uses**.

**That is now a second declaration of it**; both should collapse into one header
when `MemAlloc` lands. Recorded because the build cannot report it.

**Fact.** `SetAreaNameBoxState3` writes a word at `0xA0` of the window contents,
so the filler before `0x198` in `struct unk_0202AAA8` is split to expose it.
Nothing after it moves.

**Fact.** `GetKeyboardStringResult` reads through the unnamed pointer
`_020AFDF0`, which **keeps its placeholder name**, into a word at `0xF8` of
whatever it points at.

## `GetWindow` was looked at and deliberately left in asm

**This is the honest deferral that sets up the window cluster.**

**Fact.** It indexes `WINDOW_LIST` with a stride of `0xE0`, so its argument is an
**index** -- but `overlay_31_02382820.c` declares it as taking a
`struct Window *` and **passes one**.

**Fact.** Landing it correctly means retyping that caller, and `struct Window`
there describes only its **first 7 bytes**, so the two disagree about more than
this function.

It was therefore left rather than landed with a knowingly wrong interface. It
lands two commits later in [`aade0cdf`](aade0cdf.md).
