## Three wrappers around one helper

**Fact.** With `DoMoveParalyze__02326E80` from
[`f504e6de`](f504e6de.md), the tree now has **three** distinct wrappers around
`TryInflictParalysisStatus` with identical arguments, at three addresses. Their
pmdsky-debug names carry address suffixes precisely because the names collide.
Nothing here explains why the game has three; the bytes only establish that it
does.

## What the arguments rest on

**Fact.** `LowerSpeed` and `TryInflictParalysisStatus` are declared in
`move_orb_effects.h`, so `DoMoveLowerSpeed1` lowering speed by one stage with
the message shown, and the paralysis wrappers displaying the message and not
being check-only calls, are established rather than guessed.

**Inference.** The five `TryInflict*Status(…, TRUE, FALSE)` helpers are
undeclared in the tree; the `displayMessage` / `onlyCheck` parameter names are
carried over from `TryInflictParalysisStatus` by analogy, and the argument
*positions* match, but nothing here verifies the names.

**Deliberately not guessed.** `TryWarp(attacker, defender, 0, 0)` has its third
and fourth parameters **left unnamed** in the extern. Nothing in this commit
establishes what they are, and the `(displayMessage, onlyCheck)` analogy does
not obviously apply to a warp. A reviewer should read them as "two zeros of
unknown meaning".

## Process

Same as before: faithful context, per-function scratch scores, region-directive
check (none — US only).

**Tooling note.** `build-tools/build.sh` copies changed files into a clean clone
but has **no way to express a deletion**, and this batch deleted two `asm/*.s`
files that `extract_function.py` emptied. Since `common.mk` enumerates asm with
`$(wildcard asm/*.s)`, the stale files reappear in the clone and get assembled.
The build was run through a direct `docker run` that replicates deletions with
`rm`. `build.sh` should learn a delete list; until it does, any batch that
empties a `.s` cannot be verified through it.
