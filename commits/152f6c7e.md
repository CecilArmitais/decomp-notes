## The riskiest struct change of the three

**Fact, and the reason this needs a reviewer's eye.** The two Kecleon accessors
index arrays hanging off pointers at `0x132C` and `0x1370`. **Unlike the previous
two extensions, those offsets fall *inside* `fill2[0x1009]`** rather than past
the struct's end, so the filler is **split into three runs** with the two
pointers between them.

**Fact, verified.** `maybeMoney` still lands at `0x1394` and nothing after it
moves, **which the rebuild of every `struct bag_items` user confirms**.

Appending past the end (the earlier commits) cannot move an existing member;
splitting filler *can*. This one genuinely depended on the rebuild.

## Constants and existing definitions

**Fact.** `SetMoneyStored` clamps to `0x0098967F` -- decimal 9,999,999 -- and to
zero below.

**Fact.** `GetExclusiveItemOffset` returns zero unless the item is in
`CATEGORY_EXCLUSIVE_ITEMS`, **already defined as 15 in `item.h`**, and otherwise
the item's distance from `0x1bc`, narrowed to 16 bits.

## A deliberate refusal to name

**Fact.** `IsMonsterAffectedByGravelyrockGroundMode` normalises the species
through `FemaleToMaleForm` and compares against two ids, **left as raw values
because naming them is not this commit's job**.

**Fact.** Its return is **word-sized**: `bool8` appends `and r0, r0, #0xff`,
which the target does not have. A concrete instance of the byte-width return
diagnostic.

**Fact.** `SwapShopFreeDoublePointer` frees through two levels of indirection and
clears the outer pointer, returning early when it is already null.
