Resolve these against the tree, not from memory — the ids are PMD's internal
ordering, not National Dex. `280` is `TREECKO`, which reads as Ralts if you
assume the wrong scheme.

**Inference, but about as strong as this gets.** All 36 values are members of
the playable starter/partner roster, in 18 pairings with no pairing repeated —
what a title-screen demo team table would hold. `RandomizeDemoActors` picks
`RandInt(18)` and stores the pair into the `SPECIAL_ACTORS` demo slots, closing
the loop.

**Relevant to typing:** records 2 and 4 use `_SECONDARY` ids (883, 752 = base +
`MONSTER_GENDER_ID_OFFSET` of 600), so the field carries a *full gendered*
`monster_id`, not a bare species index.

**Name suggestions** (offered, not applied): `struct demo_team`, members `hero`
and `partner`. pmdsky-debug independently describes the region as
`18*0x4 (Hero ID 0x2, Partner ID 0x2)`. High confidence; unapplied only because
naming wasn't the task.

**Caveat.** A separate `.global`, `_020A68CA`, sits at `DEMO_TEAMS + 2` — on the
second field of record 0. Any future rename must keep both symbols resolving to
the same address.

## `RandomizeDemoActors`

Picks one of 18 demo teams and copies its two species into the `SPECIAL_ACTORS`
demo slots.

The species are **cast** to `enum monster_id` on store, because `monster_id`
declares no negative enumerator and so is unsigned under `-enum min`, which
cannot reproduce the signed (`ldrsh`) reads.

## Open question

**Should `enum monster_id` declare a `-1` sentinel?** The game does return -1
from `GetScriptEntityMonsterId`. Adding the sentinel was tested and builds —
including when applied to `struct monster`'s own `id` — so this is a question of
correct meaning, not feasibility. Left to upstream rather than diverging here.
