## How this cluster was chosen

The selection rule written up in [`4f46fed9`](4f46fed9.md) — *look for shared,
already-named types, not for files with many small functions* — was turned into
an actual scan for this batch: for every asm file, which globals do its small
functions touch, and which of those already have a typed `extern` in `include/`.

**One file came back clean.** `asm/main_02042BE0.s`, 14 small functions on
`UNK_STORAGE_STRUCT_0X14`, which this branch itself typed in
[`80c33c35`](80c33c35.md). That is the scan working as intended, and it is worth
keeping as a tool rather than a one-off.

## The struct, and why its size is trustworthy

**Fact.** `struct unk_020AFEE0` was introduced in
[`80c33c35`](80c33c35.md) holding only the pointer at `0x8` and filler before it.
Its first eight bytes are now split into what these functions actually read —
halfwords at `0x0` and `0x2`, a pointer at `0x4` — with a word at `0x10` added
past the existing end. **Nothing that already used `field_0x8` moves.**

The object behind that pointer is new. **Its size is derived, not guessed:**

- **Fact.** Its `s16` array at `0x4` is bounded by `0x3e8` in **three separate
  loops** — 1000 entries, ending at `0x7D3`.
- **Fact.** The signed byte the collection-menu wrappers read sits at `0x7D4`,
  **immediately after it**. Two independent readings agreeing on the boundary.
- **Fact.** Two further fields at `0x910` and `0x18BC` come from `sub_020430F4`.
- **Fact.** Filling the gaps brings the struct to **exactly `0x18C0`** — which is
  the size in **upstream's own name** for its allocator,
  `InitUnkStorageStruct0x18c0`.

That last agreement is the reason the layout is worth trusting: the arithmetic
lands on a number chosen by someone else, from a different source. **The field
names remain placeholders**, because nothing here establishes meaning.

## 14 of 17 matched on the first candidate

**The three that did not were all the same thing again**: loops whose index and
pointer landed in swapped registers, every instruction otherwise correct, closed
by reordering the local declarations.

That is now the **third consecutive batch** in which this was the only obstacle —
the fade handlers ([`59c4a95e`](59c4a95e.md)), four of the adventure-log
accessors ([`4f46fed9`](4f46fed9.md)), and these three. The rule in
`docs/MATCHING_TIPS.md` is holding up well.

**All orderings were enumerated rather than guessed** — two locals for
`sub_02043254` and `GetFirstSelectedStorageItemIndex`, three for
`CountSelectedStorageItems`. That is cheap and exact, and it avoids the trap of
trying one swap, seeing no change, and concluding the cause is elsewhere.

## Provisional prototypes

**Fact.** Eight callees remain asm. Their prototypes are declared in the
**shared** header rather than repeated per file, and **none of them was declared
anywhere else in the tree** (checked, not assumed).

**Two are typed from evidence rather than convention:**
`IsCollectionMenuActive` and `IsCollectionMenuState3` return `bool8` because
**both callers mask with `and r0, r0, #0xff`**. The rest are conventional and a
reviewer should treat them as guesses.
