## Decompiled

| function | address |
|---|---|
| `DseTrackEvent_SetBank` | `0x02071B60` |
| `DseTrackEvent_SetBankMsb` | `0x02071B8C` |
| `DseTrackEvent_SetKeyBend` | `0x02072184` |

**Fact.** All three assemble a 16-bit value from the event stream and hand it to
a `DseChannel_` setter, following `DseTrackEvent_SetBankLsb` in
`lib/DSE/src/main_02071BF4.c`.

## Operand order, with a measured cost

**Fact.** `SetBankLsb` replaces the low byte of `swd_id`; `SetBankMsb` is its
mirror and replaces the high byte, which is why its operands read
`(*ptr_next_byte << 8) + (channel->swd_id & 0xFF)`. **Written the other way round
the addition is emitted in the wrong order and scores 30.**

## A cast that types a still-asm callee

**Fact.** `SetBank` and `SetKeyBend` take both bytes from the stream and differ
only in the **width cast the target applies before the call** -- `lsr` for the
unsigned bank, `asr` for the signed bend. That is what types each callee's second
parameter.

Good evidence pattern: the *caller's* cast tells you the callee's signature even
when the callee is still assembly.

## A duplicate declaration flagged at the time, deliberately

**Fact.** `DseChannel_SetBank` and `DseChannel_SetKeyBend` are still asm, so
their prototypes are provisional and declared in the new headers.

**`DseChannel_SetBank` is now declared in two places** -- here and in
`lib/DSE/src/main_02071BF4.c`, which declared it from its own call site earlier.
**They agree**, but nothing in the build checks that, and the duplicate should
collapse into one header when the callee lands.

This is recorded rather than silently left, because a build genuinely cannot
catch it: the two declarations never share a translation unit.

## The tooling bug this commit works around

**Fact.** It restores four `; 0xADDRESS` label comments in
`lib/DSE/asm/main_02071AE0.s` that a previous `precommit.py` run had **stripped**.
The addresses came from the pre-split file at [`00d4d642`](00d4d642.md).

**Nothing was mismatched by their absence** -- the ROM built fine without them --
but `extract_function.py` finds a function by searching its label line for
`; 0x`, so `SetBank` and `SetBankMsb` **could not be extracted** until they were
back.

`precommit.py` was fixed to keep that form. A silent, byte-neutral tooling bug
that destroys upstream annotations is exactly the kind a matching build will
never surface.
