## Decompiled

**Nothing.** This replaces a placeholder with a real layout **supplied by the
project maintainer**, together with the four types it is built from --
`WindowTemplate`, `WindowBlock`, `WindowTile`, `CursorParams`, plus `Point`.

## Provenance, stated plainly

**The field names here are the maintainer's, not derived in this commit.** The
offsets and widths are checkable against the asm; **the names are not, and should
be read as supplied rather than proven.**

That distinction is the whole point of this note: everything below labelled
*fact* is a check that was actually run, and the naming is not among them.

## Four independent checks the layout satisfies

1. **`sizeof` is `0xE0`** -- the stride `GetWindow` multiplies by.
2. **`is_active` is an `s8` at `0xB6`** -- which is where `DeleteWindow` does
   `ldrnesb`.
3. **`WindowTemplate.width` is at `0x06`** -- where the previous partial struct,
   inherited from `overlay_31_02382820.h`, already had `width`.
4. **`x`, `y`, `width`, `height` at `0x04`-`0x07` account for
   `GetWindowRectangle` exactly**: it writes `y*8`, `y*8 + height*8`, `x*8` and
   `x*8 + width*8`, so its output is top, bottom, left, right. That function is
   **rewritten against the named fields** rather than shifting anonymous bytes.

**A fifth check arrived later**, in [`7f6977e2`](7f6977e2.md): four bss
`.global`s inside `WINDOW_LIST` fall **exactly** on this struct's field
boundaries, and the block measures `0xB8 + 0x10C8 = 0x1180 = 20 * 0xE0`. That is
corroboration from the linker rather than from the struct definition, and it is
the strongest of the five.

## Three choices worth reviewing

- **The struct keeps its tag**, so both `struct Window` and `Window` resolve and
  **no existing use changes**.
- **`GetWindowContents` still returns `void *`** rather than the `u32` the field
  is typed as, because **all 22 of its callers assign the result to a pointer**;
  the cast is a no-op and avoids 22 integer-to-pointer conversions. A reviewer
  may prefer the honest `u32` plus casts.
- **`width` moving inside the template** means `overlay_31_02382820.c` reads
  `window2->template.width` -- the one source change the layout forces.
