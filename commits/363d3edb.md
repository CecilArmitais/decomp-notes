**Fact.** `GetSelectedMenuItemIdx` computes the absolute item index from the
paging struct added in [`75c76fa7`](75c76fa7.md) -- current page times items per
page, plus the selection within the page -- **which the target folds into one
`mla`**.

## Two identical bodies, kept identical again

**Fact.** `GetWindowIdSelectedMenuItemIdx` and `GetAdvancedMenuCurrentOption`
have identical bodies and are **decompiled as written rather than one calling the
other**, since that is what the target does.

Same decision as the `SetBpm`/`SetBpm2` pair in [`0888986a`](0888986a.md). It is
worth being consistent about: de-duplicating would change the bytes.

## Struct growth by filler split

**byte at `0x8`** and a **word at `0x1B0`**. The filler before `0x198` is split
to expose the first **without moving anything after it**.
