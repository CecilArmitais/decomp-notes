## The cluster deferred in `c4338759` is closed

**Fact.** [`c4338759`](c4338759.md) dropped eight functions that differed from
their targets only in instruction *order* around a call with a stack argument.
Declaring the stat globals `const` matches all eight.

**Inference, but with a clear mechanism.** Setting up a stack argument *stores
to memory*. With a plain `extern`, MWCC cannot prove the global is unaffected by
those stores, so its load stays below them; `const` says it cannot change and
the load hoists — which is retail's schedule. The four spellings falsified last
time (a `StatIndex` temporary, literal `1` instead of `TRUE`, both, split
declaration) all failed because none of them touched aliasing.

Decompiled here: `DoMoveGrowl`, `DoMoveLowerDefense1`, `DoMoveFeatherDance`,
`DoMoveFakeTears`, `DoMoveMetalSound`, `DoMoveScreech`, `DoMoveCharm`,
`DoMoveHealOrder`, plus `LogMessageQuiet` and `LogMessageWithPopup`.

## A correction carried from the previous batch

**Fact.** `DoMoveMetalSound` lowers by **3** stages. The candidate written in
`c4338759` assumed 2 by analogy with `DoMoveFakeTears`, and the asm says
`mov r3, #3`. It was never landed with the wrong value — it was in the dropped
eight — but the wrong constant sat in that batch's spec, and only reading the
body caught it. **Analogy is not evidence**, even inside a family where nine
neighbours share a shape.

## The const change is tree-wide, and that is the interesting part

**Fact.** A global cannot be `const` in one translation unit and not another, so
every existing `extern struct StatIndex` in `src/` was const-ified — thirteen
files, all containing functions that already matched.

**The ROM still matches.** That is the part worth weighing: the change is not a
local trick that happens to fix eight functions, it is a claim about the
symbols, and every previously-matching caller survives it. A reviewer who
doubts the `const` reading has thirteen files' worth of evidence against the
doubt.

**Still unverified**: whether the *data* symbols are actually emitted into a
read-only section in the ROM. Nothing here checks that; the evidence is entirely
about how callers schedule around them.
